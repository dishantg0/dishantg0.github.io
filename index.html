About

Engineer first, storyteller second.

I started out writing data pipelines and training classical models, and grew into leading AI engineering end to end: research spikes, product discovery, model evaluation and the slow work of making systems reliable in production.

My focus today is large language models, retrieval, agents and the tooling that keeps them honest: evaluation harnesses, guardrails and observability. I care about teams that ship steadily and systems that people can trust.

The Role

What a Lead AI Engineer does, in my day to day

The job sits between research, product and platform. Most weeks touch all four of these.

01

Architecture & Strategy

I decide how AI fits into the product: which problems need a model, which need a rule, and how LLMs, retrieval and existing services fit together.

02

Building & Shipping

Hands-on with prototypes, fine-tuning, prompt and agent design, and the APIs that take a model from a notebook to real users.

03

Quality & Safety

Setting up evaluation suites, red-teaming, guardrails and monitoring so we know when a model gets better, worse or just different.

04

Leading People

Mentoring engineers, running design reviews, hiring, and translating AI trade-offs for product and leadership stakeholders.

“Good AI engineering is mostly good engineering, plus a healthy respect for uncertainty.”
